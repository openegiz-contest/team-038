The mine: from the face to copper in concentrate

3 block clusters→3 shovels→8 haul trucks on 12 road segments→ primary crusher / ROM pad / waste dump→coarse ore stockpile→concentrator feed

Support systems

  • Dispatcher: truck allocation, ore routing, blending, fuel rationing
  • Maintenance planner: wear-dependent failure risk, planned stops
  • Fuel bay: diesel stock, deliveries, refuelling queue
  • Power grid: crusher and concentrator demand, 15-min peak, grid cap
  • Scenario controller: what-ifs and module switches

Two copies

The live copy takes the what-ifs and switches. The baseline copy runs the same seed with every module on and no what-if. Each entity draws from its own random stream, so live − baseline = the effect.

Behaviour: physics, data and conservation

  • Trucks: Cat 793F class (226.8 t, 1,848 kW). Speed comes from the power balance on each segment's grade and rolling resistance, with retarder limits downhill. Diesel is 0.25 L/kWh plus idle burn.
  • Shovels: bucket size × fill × loose density, with swing cycles and spotting.
  • Ore: block grades follow the 2,000 GeoMet copper assays and Bond work indices its 60 grindability tests. The spatial pattern is synthetic.
  • Energy: Bond's law gives crusher kWh/t = 0.0129·Wi and concentrator kWh/t = 0.79·Wi, plus a 2.5 MW base load. Crusher capacity is limited by its 750 kW motor at the current hardness.
  • Recovery: 87.9 %, the mean of the 53 GeoMet flotation tests.
  • Failures: hazard λ0·exp(k(1 − health/100)), drawn from each machine's own random stream.
  • Queues: shovels, crusher pocket, ROM and dump tip spots, fuel bay and workshop bays; roads have capacity limits.

What-if: crusher failure

  • One click (or POST /api/scenarios/crusher_failure) writes the scenario-controller twin.
  • The live crusher stops (red ring). The dispatcher sends ore to the ROM pad, the coarse ore stockpile drains, and plant feed and copper fall.
  • The baseline keeps running; the KPI table shows the gap.
  • The smoke test runs this round trip. In one run live crushing fell 584 t/h below the baseline.
  • Undo restores the previous state, and the gap closes as the crusher catches up.
  • Resync live = baseline copies the baseline state into the live copy, so the next what-if starts from identical mines.

Decision modules: logged decisions, on/off switch

  • Haulage and dispatch: each empty truck goes to the shovel with the earliest expected loading start, corrected for the target-rate deficit and the feed grade. Ore goes to the ROM pad when the crusher is down or the grade is off band. Re-routing and fuel rationing are included. Off: fixed 2/2/4 allocation, all ore to the crusher.
  • Maintenance planner: plans a stop when P(fail in 8 h) × MTTR × loss × 1.3 > PM time × loss, and builds coarse ore stock before crusher maintenance. Off: run to failure.
  • Energy manager: under a grid cap it throttles the concentrator to stay below the cap. Off: a 15-min block over the cap trips the plant for 15 min.

Each decision is published to the module's twin (decision_log) with its reason. A switch writes the scenario-controller twin and affects the live copy only, so the KPI deltas show what the module is worth.

cd team-038
./twinvision.sh up        # platform + TwinVision

./twinvision.sh test      # 46 unit tests in the image
  • A clean Ubuntu 24.04 host (Docker 29.1, Compose 2.40, no make) needs no manual steps: 122 s to start, then all checks pass.
  • The platform's own make smoke also passes.

Limitations

  • 2D map only; there is no Unity scene and no vision module.
  • The concentrator is a feed sink with Bond energy and constant recovery.
  • The site is fictional. Only the grade, hardness and recovery distributions come from real data.
  • The simulator restarts from t = 0 with its container; scenario state persists in Ditto.
